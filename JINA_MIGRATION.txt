JINA COST NOTE
  Free tier   : one-time 10M token grant (no monthly refill) -- lasts ~3 weeks
                at our volume, then stops.
  Pay-as-you-go: ~$0.05 per 1M tokens ($50 per 1B). No subscription required.
  Our usage   : ~0.5M tokens/day (10-30K per scraped page x weekday runs)
                = ~10M tokens/month = ~$0.50/month.
  Takeaway    : Jina is effectively cheap. A ~$5 token top-up runs most of a
                year. Keeping the ~6 gated companies on Jina is cheaper and
                less fragile than building CSRF/bot-protected scrapers or
                self-hosting for a clean IP. Migrate only the easy wins above.
